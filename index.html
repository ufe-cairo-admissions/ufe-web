Admissions Process

  • Online Pre-Registration: Complete this form to generate your applicant reference number.
  • Document Review: Bring your birth certificate, national ID/passport, and high school transcripts to El Shorouk Campus.
  • Language Placement: Sit for the English and French placement assessment to determine your starting language level.
  • Final Enrollment: Confirm your seat and complete tuition registration.
